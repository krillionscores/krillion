Time to Krill

How deep the group goes in Krillion’s daily dive. Scores are taken from the screenshots posted in the chat and updated each weekday morning.

Tap a name in the leaderboard to follow that player through the page. Zones use the real ocean depth zones: twilight from 200 m, midnight from 1,000 m, the abyss from 4,000 m and the hadal zone from 6,000 m. The faint shapes are mountains turned upside down: Ben Nevis (1,345 m), Mount Olympus (2,918 m), Mount Fuji (3,776 m), Mont Blanc (4,806 m) and Kilimanjaro (5,895 m), each shown when its summit falls within the depths on screen.

Each point is 10 m of depth, so a perfect 700-point dive reaches 7,000 m. A win means the top score on a dive that at least two people played, and tied scores share the win. A streak counts consecutive daily dives.
